Privacy Policy

LiveChatTool uses authentication tokens provided by supported platforms to access features authorized by the user.

LiveChatTool does not require users to provide their platform passwords directly to the application.

Authentication credentials and tokens used by the desktop application may be stored locally on the user's device for the purpose of maintaining authenticated sessions.

LiveChatTool does not sell user data.

Third-party services such as TikTok and YouTube may process information according to their own privacy policies when users authenticate or use their APIs.
